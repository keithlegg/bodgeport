Bridgeport Series I V2XT 
    
Spindle Taper: #30 QC (Erickson Quick Change) will work with NMTB#30
Search terms: ("NMTB 30" OR "erickson quick change" OR "quick change 30")

spindle RPM  - low is 60-450 , high is 500-4200
travel is 30" in the X axis, 12" in the Y axis, and 5" in the Z (quill)


use of compression sleeves on motor output shaft to drive ball screw. 
no shaft key---only friction compression locked up the drive sprocket, had slippage problems
